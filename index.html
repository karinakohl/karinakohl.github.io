About

Karina Kohl is a professor at the Institute of Informatics, UFRGS, and researcher at the SEResearch Lab. Her work focuses on the human and social dimensions of software engineering: how developers experience their work, how AI is reshaping technical practice, and how teams can be made more inclusive.
